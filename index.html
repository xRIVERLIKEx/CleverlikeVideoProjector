    How to use it in Minecraft
    1. Download add-on and open the .mcaddon file. Minecraft Education imports it.
    2. Create or edit a world. Under Behavior Packs, turn on the add-on.
    3. Use the Video Projector item and pick a video. Face a wall and the screen hangs flat on it.
    4. Tap the projector to play, pause, start over, turn, move, resize, set the sound, switch the lights or remove it. A redstone lamp under it works as a switch.

    Prototype. Sound is saved as OGG pieces (WAV if the converter can't load), and pausing jumps back to the start of a sound piece. Test 256 colors and the screen glow in your Minecraft Education version before sharing with a class.

    Colors
    Fewer colors, smaller add-on. Cartoons, slides and Minecraft footage often look the same at 128 or 64. Compare in the In-game look.
    Steady colors
    Pixels that barely change keep last frame's color. Much smaller, with very slow fades moving in small steps.
    Smooths banding in skies and fades, at a cost of size.
    Clean up grain
    Skip repeated frames
    Frames that barely change are stored once and held on screen.
    Shrinks by picking pixels instead of blending, so pixel art, Minecraft skins and text stay crisp.
    Screen
    Starting width
    Players can change it in game with the projector's Size button.
    Light blocks in front of the screen light it and the room around it.
    The picture itself shines, even in pitch dark. Makes the add-on bigger (about 2x with 256 colors or fewer).
    Sound
    Sound quality
    Small suits speech, Standard most videos, Best music.
    Sound pieces
    Longer pieces make the sound much smaller. Pausing jumps back to the start of a piece. Silent parts are left out.
    Heard from
    Players can change volume and distance in game with the Sound button.
